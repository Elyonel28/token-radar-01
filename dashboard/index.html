How the radar prices a launch

implied = TVL × multiple × growth × maturity

  • multiple — the median FDV/TVL of the live-token protocols in the protocol's category (at least 3 comparables; otherwise the global median). Capped at 1.5. The market's own pricing, not a hardcoded list of names.
  • growth — 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −50%, +100%) × 0.35. A hot week lifts value; it never doubles it.
  • maturity — 0.70 + 0.30 × ln(1 + age in days) / ln(1 + 1095). Older protocols are worth more, with diminishing returns. Unknown age counts as 365 days.

What the board covers

The snapshot holds 758 protocols at or above $5M TVL. The board ranks all 198 that record no token and are not a wrapped, staked or bridged product, an exchange or a versioned pool: the 40 on the house watchlist (TVL at or above $50M, no token, not a wrapped, staked or bridged product) plus 158 more below the floor. The watchlist is a floor, never a ceiling. Live-token protocols are kept apart and shown at the market's own value.

How to farm

Open any protocol for a six-step plan: a fresh wallet, the main position, a second qualifying action, one action a week, a hold of 30–60 days, and a claim only from the protocol's own page. Each step states what it costs and how long it takes. Never pay to claim, and never trust a link that is not the protocol's own.
